Privacy policy for Geoff's Tools

Geoff's Tools is a private Google sign-in app that Geoff Wright uses to work with his own Google Docs, Sheets, Drive files, and Apps Script projects. It is not offered to the public.

Only Geoff signs in to it. It reads and changes files in his Google account, either directly or through the Claude AI assistant working at his request, so Anthropic may process that content to carry out the request. It does not collect, sell, or share anyone else's data.
